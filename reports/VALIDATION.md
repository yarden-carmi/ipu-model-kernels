# qbench.ipu vs the IPU emulator

Does qbench's simulation of the IPU compute what the IPU computes? Generated by
`python -m ipu_kernelgen.validate` (ipu-kernelgen) against ipu-emulator.

FP32 softmax differs by at most one FP32 rounding: the order its 128 lanes are summed in.

Before qbench.ipu mirrored the kernels store by store (commit 'SiLU and softmax mirror the zoo kernels'),
SiLU was 46-97% identical (up to 64 steps in dynamic) and softmax 26-87% (up to 8 steps): the methods
rounded different intermediates and used a different log2(e) than the kernels.

## 2. Stage by stage: the MobileViT L5 transformer layer (FP32)

The layer chain of `kernels/mobilevit/mobilevit_layer.py` (query-major attention, scale 1/sqrt(head_dim)
folded into Q) runs on the emulator's kernels; the same layer as a PyTorch module runs through
`ApproxSimulator`. Max abs difference from the emulator at each stage boundary:

| stage | qbench exact (eps=1e-5) | qbench as the kernels (no eps, exp2 softmax) |
|---|---:|---:|
| ln1 | 4.3e-05 | 4.8e-07 |
| qkv | 0.00017 | 4.8e-06 |
| scores | 0.00092 | 1.7e-05 |
| softmax | 2.4e-05 | 2.9e-06 |
| attn_v | 0.00028 | 2.6e-05 |
| out_proj | 0.00066 | 5.3e-05 |
| resid1 | 0.00066 | 5.3e-05 |
| ln2 | 9.2e-05 | 1.1e-05 |
| ffn1 | 0.00019 | 2.5e-05 |
| ffn2 | 0.00065 | 6.6e-05 |
| resid2 | 0.0011 | 6.9e-05 |

With the kernels' semantics (LayerNorm without eps, Q57; exp2 softmax) the first stage agrees to FP32
rounding; what remains grows through the layer from the order the emulator's matmuls sum their FP32
products, magnified by the attention scores.
